## 💡 Key Design Decisions

### Why Different Backgrounds?
- **Engagement**: Each page feels fresh and unique
- **Navigation**: Visual cues help users understand location

- **Performance**: CSS animations are lightweight

- **User Feedback**: Clear visual responses to interactions

- **Professional**: Polished, app-like feel
- **Accessibility**: Visual feedback helps all users
